Limits, stated properly

  1. The attacks are synthetic: the injector writes them into real RINEX observables from a static reference station. The route, the vehicles and the environments are a presentation frame; only the measured displacement between believed and true position separates the tracks.
  2. One station, one day, one injector, open sky. Thresholds are measured separation points for USN8 on 2026-08-20 and generalise no further than that sentence.
  3. Headline numbers above are computed from the streams on disk at server start and carry their source. Where a stream is missing the value is blank, never typed in.
  4. Operator takeover moves a vehicle in the presentation frame under a human's command; ARBITER itself never commands motion. Automatic corrections act on the corrected fix and are gated by its protection level.
